# Development direction

Build an ecosystem where increasingly capable behavior emerges through survival and reproduction. The nursery and frontier environment is the starting point for future work.

## Foundation

- Independent spiking brains act on local sensory information.
- Feeding funds metabolism, movement, growth, and reproduction.
- Descendants inherit brain topology, neural parameters, mass, and diet.
- Weather, terrain, moving resources, crowding, and predation create ecological pressures.
- Seeded worlds, exact checkpoint continuation, and detailed replays support experiments.

## Next questions

1. Can lineages sustain themselves through repeated environmental cycles and expand beyond the nursery?
2. Which pressures encourage useful memory, navigation, cooperation, and predator/prey behavior?
3. How do mutation rates and neural/body costs affect complexity and lineage persistence?
4. Where does profiling show opportunities to simulate larger populations or longer histories?

Keep tuning accessible in code and test mechanical invariants separately from ecological outcomes. Develop new pressures gradually and compare repeated seeds. Future population-support mechanisms should be designed from observed needs; previous fitness selection, archive, and immigration systems are retired.
